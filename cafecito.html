The project

Working behind the bar while contributing to marketing gave me a firsthand perspective on Cafecito Social's culture, customers, and everyday rhythm. I created Instagram content that highlighted the products, the people, and the community behind the brand.

Creative approach

Beyond the coffee cup: everyday details, candid community moments, and short-form video that communicated a warm, human brand personality.

What I learned

I learned that a café's personality isn't just in its branding deck. It's in the regular who orders the usual, the rush that somehow starts five minutes before you're ready, and the tiny details people remember. Working both behind the bar and behind the camera taught me to notice those moments instead of staging everything. Yes, I learned to make coffee. More importantly, I learned how to make a place feel like somewhere people belong. That's the kind of storytelling I want to keep doing.
