What I learned

Greystar taught me that community doesn't magically appear because a building has a nice lobby and a calendar of events. You have to earn it: remember names, listen when something goes wrong, and show up even when the turnout isn't exactly a sold-out concert. Between leasing tours, resident events, and social content, I learned how to turn everyday interactions into relationships. It also made me realize I genuinely enjoy bringing people together—even when that means juggling a hundred little details behind the scenes.
