The project

At Sproos!, I supported social-first initiatives including Washington Square Park street interviews, creator research, and campaign coordination. The work focused on connecting with younger audiences through approachable, participatory content.

Creative approach

Bring brand conversations into public spaces through questions, trivia, and spontaneous interactions. I supported content ideation, production, outreach, and deliverables.

What I learned

Sproos! taught me that the best content sometimes starts with walking up to a complete stranger and hoping they don't pretend they have somewhere urgent to be. Street interviews pushed me to think on my feet, listen, and make people comfortable fast. Creator outreach taught me the less glamorous side of creativity: follow-ups, deadlines, logistics, and the occasional spreadsheet that deserves its own Oscar. I came away loving the mix of spontaneity and strategy—and realizing that good marketing is a conversation, not a monologue.
