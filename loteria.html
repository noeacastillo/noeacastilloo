What I learned

This one felt especially personal. Bringing Lotería—a game tied to so many familiar tables, voices, and memories—into a New York community event reminded me that culture isn't a decorative theme you add at the end. It's the reason people connect in the first place. I learned to think about events less as a perfect run-of-show and more as a room full of people who should feel invited to join in. Also, nothing brings out everyone's competitive side quite like a game of Lotería.
